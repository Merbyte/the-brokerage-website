# The Brokerage Advisory — Design Inspiration Pack

## Purpose

These references communicate the desired visual quality, composition and interaction direction for The Brokerage Advisory website.

They are not templates to copy.

The build must be original. Do not reproduce any reference site’s:
- Layout structure
- Copywriting
- Photography
- Logos or branding
- Exact colour values
- Component design
- Icons
- Motion patterns
- Code
- Page hierarchy
- Investment, property or financial claims

The Brokerage Advisory must remain a premium Singapore financing-advisory website. Trust, clarity, accessibility, compliance and conversion take priority over visual novelty.

---

## Project visual target

Create a website that feels:

- Premium and editorial
- Calm, credible and commercially mature
- Sophisticated without being intimidating
- High-trust rather than high-hype
- Modern but not generic fintech
- Bespoke but not overly luxurious
- Clear on desktop and mobile

The design must avoid:

- Generic AI SaaS landing-page styling
- Crypto, trading or speculative-investment aesthetics
- Consumer-loan “instant approval” visuals
- Casino-like gold/black styling
- Excessive gradients, glow, glassmorphism or animation
- Repetitive bento grids and card-heavy layouts
- Fake dashboards, counters, ratings or social proof
- Unverified finance data or claims

- Business/project cash-flow explanations
- The “direct bank versus advisory” comparison

# Screenshot Reference — Financing Route Comparison

## Use this reference for

- Section structure:
  - Small eyebrow label
  - Large serif heading
  - Concise explanatory copy
  - Comparison table
  - Disclaimer/update note
  - CTA below the table
- Dark table header against warm light background
- Clear decision-oriented hierarchy

## Translation for The Brokerage Advisory

Build a qualitative comparison table:

| Financing Route | May Be Relevant For | What Usually Needs Assessing | Next Step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business Financing | Growth, operations and expansion | Business profile, cash flow, existing facilities and documents | Discuss your situation |
| Trade Financing | Supplier payments and receivables timing | Invoices, contracts, counterparties and cycle | Discuss cash flow |
| Property-Backed Financing | Asset-backed or larger needs | Ownership, valuation, existing debt and repayment capacity | Speak to a specialist |
| Project Financing | Contract execution and mobilisation | Contract, payment timing and supporting documents | Discuss your project |

Do not publish unverified rates, financing limits, LTVs, eligibility rules or timelines in this comparison module.

# Screenshot Reference — Direct Bank vs Advisory

## Use this reference for

- Strong dark-background contrast section
- Clear side-by-side comparison
- Concise bullet hierarchy
- Decision support framing
- Use of gold as a positive accent and muted red for cautions

## Translation for The Brokerage Advisory

Use a compliance-safe comparison.

### Going Direct

- Start with a familiar product or institution
- Work through one institution’s process first
- Discover information or documentation gaps later
- Reassess after a mismatch or decline

### With The Brokerage Advisory

- Discuss the funding purpose before selecting a route
- Review relevant facility and lender-fit considerations
- Identify information gaps that may affect the next step
- Compare possible routes across banks and financiers in our local and overseas network
- Get a clearer view of possible next steps

Do not use:

- “Ex-banker team knows every lender’s criteria”
- “Guaranteed better terms”
- “Full credit narrative before any document”
- “We will fix every decline”
- “No deal too complex”
- Any statement that implies approval, speed or financing certainty

# Implementation Rules for Claude

## Before designing

1. Read:
   - `docs/master-website-brief.md`
   - `docs/claims-and-compliance-rules.md`
   - This file
2. Run Hallmark in study mode.
3. Create `docs/design-reference-study.md`.
4. Extract design DNA without copying.

## During design

Use:
- UI UX Pro Max for component system, accessibility and responsive UX
- Taste Skill for premium editorial interpretation
- Frontend Design skill for implementation
- Design Motion Principles only after the layout is stable

Recommended visual settings:
- Design variance: 5/10
- Motion intensity: 3/10
- Visual density: 4/10

## Before launch

Run Hallmark and Anti-Slop audit for:
- Overuse of cards
- Generic AI landing-page patterns
- Excessive gradients, pills, glow and glass effects
- Inspiration-copying risk
- Mobile layout quality
- Accessibility
- Financial-services trust
- Disclaimer visibility
- Originality
