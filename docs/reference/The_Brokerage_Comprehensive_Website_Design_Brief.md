# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

## 1.1 Objective

Design and build a premium, modern website for **The Brokerage Advisory**, a Singapore-based financing advisory focused on diagnosing business financing situations before recommending a financing route.

The website must not feel like a generic loan comparison site or aggressive loan broker.

The intended perception is:

> **A sophisticated Singapore financing advisory firm that understands difficult credit situations, diagnoses the underlying problem, gives straight answers, and connects clients to appropriate financing routes across banks, financiers and capital providers.**

The website primarily supports the company's existing cold-outreach sales process by giving prospects a strong reason to trust the company before or after a sales call. Inbound lead generation is a secondary objective, but the architecture must be SEO/AEO-ready so the website can develop into an inbound acquisition channel over time.

# 2. FOUNDER-APPROVED STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

The following positioning decisions come from the completed Founder Brand, Positioning & Website Direction Decision Pack and should be treated as the current strategic source of truth unless the founder explicitly changes them.

## 2.1 What is The Brokerage?

> **A financing advisory that diagnoses a business's situation first — then, where needed, structures capital solutions for complex or non-standard cases such as multiple entities, unusual collateral or prior rejections.**

The founder describes the business as a blend of **Financing Advisory** and **Capital Solutions Partner**.

## 2.2 What should The Brokerage be known for?

> **The firm people call when they have a credit or financing problem no one else could solve — and get straight answers, not a sales pitch.**

## 2.3 Vision

> **To become Singapore's most trusted financing advisory — known for solving what others can't, without ever compromising on integrity, transparency or fair fees.**

## 2.4 Mission

> **Fact-find thoroughly on the first call, then pull documents and run bank checks to establish the client's real standing with financiers before recommending a path forward.**

The website should visualise and explain this method.

## 2.5 Primary customer

For the next 12–24 months:

- Traditional construction and engineering companies
- Owner-led businesses
- Owners approximately 40–50+
- Annual revenue approximately S$5M–S$10M

This does not mean the website should reject other businesses. Construction and engineering should simply have a particularly strong presence in messaging, examples and future content.

## 2.6 Top customer problems

1. **Growth funding:** a real growth opportunity, such as a new project, but insufficient capital/cash flow.
2. **Bank rejection:** a bank has already said no.
3. **Financing optimisation:** poor financing terms or poor advice from a previous broker.

## 2.7 Core differentiation

### Versus a bank

- Unbiased across banks and financiers
- Advice across multiple financing sources rather than one institution's products
- Focus on the client's situation rather than a single lender's product

### Versus a typical broker

- Honest advice
- Transparent communication
- Fair pricing
- No overpromising
- No underdelivering
- Genuine effort to deliver results

## 2.8 Competitive position

**Complex Financing Specialist with Strategic Financing Advisor characteristics.**

Core process:

**Assess → Diagnose → Structure → Approach the right financier**

## 2.9 Credibility pillars

1. Strong network with funders/financiers across the market
2. Deep technical expertise and ability to properly assess a case
3. Reputation for honesty and fair pricing

Do not invent numerical proof points unless the company can substantiate them.

## 2.10 Brand personality

- Professional
- Genuine
- Reliable
- Expert

Desired client feeling:

> **Reassured**

## 2.11 Tone

Primary tone: **Advisory**  
Secondary edge: **Premium**

The voice should be experienced, consultative, clear, calm, direct and commercially aware.

Avoid hype, aggressive sales language, fake urgency, guaranteed-approval language, excessive financial jargon and cheap lead-broker language.

## 2.12 Brand direction

The founder prefers:

> **Premium Financial Advisory**

Black and gold should communicate authority, sophistication, trust, premium service, bespoke attention and financial expertise.

Avoid a casino/nightclub, luxury-retail, flashy-crypto, or generic consumer-loan aesthetic.

# 27. AEO STRATEGY

The content should be easy for search engines and AI systems to understand and quote accurately.

## 27.1 Answer-first structure

Use:

**Question-style heading → direct answer → explanation → relevant considerations → CTA**

Example:

### What should I do if my business loan is rejected?

Start with a clear answer in 1–3 sentences, then explain possible reasons, considerations and next steps.

## 27.2 Standalone content blocks

Every major section should make sense if extracted independently.

Avoid vague headings such as:

- Benefits
- Solutions
- Eligibility
- Process

Prefer:

- Who is working-capital financing suitable for?
- Why might a bank reject a business loan?
- How does property equity financing work?
- What information does a financing advisor need?

## 27.3 Entity consistency

Use a consistent company description across:

- Website
- LinkedIn
- Founder profile
- Directories
- Partner websites
- Media/PR
- Review platforms

The firm assesses each client's funding requirements and matches them with appropriate banks, financial institutions and private capital providers across business, trade, property and complex financing situations.**

Shortened versions should preserve the same core meaning.
