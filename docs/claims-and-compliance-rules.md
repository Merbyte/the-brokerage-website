# Claims and Compliance Rules

## Approved public statements

- The Brokerage Advisory is a Singapore-based independent financing advisory.
- We help businesses understand their financing situation before deciding where and how to approach banks, financiers or capital providers.
- We support business, trade, property, project and complex financing situations.
- Construction and engineering is a priority client segment.
- We work with banks and financiers in our local and overseas network.
- Get a Free Financing Assessment.
- A free financing assessment is an initial discussion only. It is not a financing approval, offer, commitment or guarantee.

## Do not publish

- Any public fee statement, including “success fee only” or “zero upfront fees.”
- Guaranteed approval, rates, financing, savings, terms, timing or outcomes.
- S$100M+ placed.
- A high success rate or any approval-rate percentage.
- “Ex-banker team” unless founder-approved biographies and proof are added.
- Named lender partnerships, lender logos or client logos without permission.
- Specific rates, LTVs, loan limits, tenures or eligibility criteria without a dated review and approval.
- Invented case studies, testimonials, credentials, awards or team biographies.

## Placeholders before launch

- Exact regulatory disclaimer.
- Privacy policy.
- Terms and conditions.
- Lead destination email/CRM.
- Logo and approved image assets.
- Team profiles and case studies, if used.
