# Project & Private Capital — Page Override

**URL** `/project-private-capital/` · **Archetype** Contract-to-capital timeline · Inherits `MASTER.md` and `design.md`

## Page goal
Serve the visitor whose opportunity has outgrown their current financing structure, and make clear that larger or non-standard situations are assessed differently rather than simply refused.

## Primary visitor
An owner or director with a contract, project or opportunity larger than their existing facilities support. May involve multiple entities, unusual collateral or a structure conventional banking does not fit.

## Intent
Serious and specific. This visitor usually has a real transaction in hand and limited patience for generalities.

## Page archetype
**Contract-to-capital timeline.** The page follows a single arc: an opportunity arrives, the existing structure does not cover it, complexity increases, and the assessment widens accordingly.

## Primary visual mechanism
An **escalation ladder** — a vertical sequence of rungs where each step up represents greater structural complexity, from a single contract against existing facilities, through project-specific structuring, to alternative and private capital. Each rung names what changes at that level and what additional information is needed. Reads as ascent, distinct from Trade's cycle and Construction's timeline.

## Section order
1. Header
2. **H1 + lead** — the opportunity outgrowing the structure. No eyebrow.
3. **Escalation ladder** — lead content and primary mechanism.
4. **Where complexity comes from** — multiple entities, unusual collateral, contract execution risk, timing, prior decline.
5. **Project execution and mobilisation** — what typically needs funding before payment arrives.
6. **Alternative and private capital** — described as routes that may be relevant, with no implication of availability.
7. **Cross-border and strategic situations** — case-by-case supporting capability only; explicitly not a core service.
8. **What usually needs assessing** — contract, payment terms, timing, supporting documents, existing obligations.
9. **Route comparison** — shared qualitative table.
10. **Final CTA** — dark.
11. Footer

## CTA placement
Header; after the escalation ladder; after the route comparison; final band. Four placements. Secondary "Discuss Your Project" permitted once.

## Mobile-specific behaviour
The ladder is already vertical; rungs become full-width blocks with their headings retained. No horizontal scroll. Route comparison uses the block pattern.

## Components used
Header · Mobile nav · Escalation ladder (timeline component, contract-to-capital configuration) · Route-comparison table and mobile blocks · CTA band · Footer · Buttons · Links

## Components intentionally not used
Problem-set cards · Cash-conversion cycle · Consideration stack · FAQ accordion (kept to the FAQ page for this one) · Inline calculator · Photography

## SEO / AEO structure
H1 names the mismatch between opportunity and structure. Question-form H2s on project financing, contract financing and private capital. Service schema. Title direction: *Project & Private Capital Singapore | The Brokerage Advisory*.

## Claim / compliance safeguards
**No "we will fund" language.** No deal sizes, no minimum or maximum amounts, no rates, no timelines. M&A and cross-border remain a supporting capability, never a headline service. No named funds, sponsors or counterparties. No implication that complexity is always solvable.
