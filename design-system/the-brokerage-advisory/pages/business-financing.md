# Business Financing — Page Override

**URL** `/business-financing/` · **Archetype** Diagnostic decision framework · Inherits `MASTER.md` and `design.md`

## Page goal
Move a visitor from "I need a business loan" to "I need to understand my situation first" — and then to an assessment enquiry.

## Primary visitor
An established Singapore SME owner arriving with a product in mind (a loan) rather than a diagnosis. Often referred or cold-contacted; may have been declined elsewhere.

## Intent
Product-shopping. The page's job is to reframe without dismissing the visitor's framing.

## Page archetype
**Diagnostic decision framework.** Organised as a set of *situations*, each paired with what would need assessing. Breadth is the subject: the visitor should see that "business financing" is several different conversations.

## Primary visual mechanism
A **situation → what needs assessing** pairing list. Each entry is a two-part block: the situation in the visitor's own words, then the assessment considerations beneath. Hairline rules separate entries. No cards, no icons, no grid.

## Section order
1. Header
2. **H1 + lead** — reframes from product to situation. No eyebrow.
3. **Situation set** — the pairing list. Six to eight situations: growth opportunity, working-capital pressure, term need, existing-facility fit, refinancing consideration, prior decline, multiple entities, non-standard structure.
4. **What usually needs assessing** — the shared inputs across situations, as a plain list.
5. **What we do not do** — short honesty block: not a lender, no guaranteed outcome, assessment is a discussion.
6. **Information to prepare** — what a first conversation typically covers.
7. **Route comparison** — shared qualitative table.
8. **FAQ** — business-specific questions.
9. **Final CTA** — dark.
10. Footer

## CTA placement
Header; after the situation set; after the route comparison; final band. Four placements. Secondary "Discuss Your Situation" permitted inline within the situation set.

## Mobile-specific behaviour
Pairing list stacks naturally — situation heading, then assessment considerations. Route comparison uses the block pattern. No two-column layout survives below 768px.

## Components used
Header · Mobile nav · Service-pathway index (cross-links) · Route-comparison table and mobile blocks · FAQ accordion · CTA band · Footer · Buttons · Links · Alert block (reviewed date, if used)

## Components intentionally not used
Problem-set cards (home only) · Timeline diagram · Inline calculator · Two-column comparison · Photography

## SEO / AEO structure
H1 states that business financing starts with the situation, not a product. Question-form H2s. Service schema. Title direction: *Business Financing Singapore | The Brokerage Advisory*. Answer-first: each situation opens with a one-sentence direct statement.

## Claim / compliance safeguards
No amounts, rates, tenures or eligibility criteria. No "approved" or "we will fund" language. Prior-decline situations described without implying a decline can be reversed. Approved network wording only.

## Placeholder status
None required. If a proof module is later proposed it is omitted until `APPROVED CASE STUDY REQUIRED` is resolved.
