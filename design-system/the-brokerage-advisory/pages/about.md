# About — Page Override

**URL** `/about/` · **Archetype** Philosophy and operating-principles narrative · Inherits `MASTER.md` and `design.md`

## Page goal
Carry credibility through stated principles and method, in the complete absence of a proof layer. The hardest page on the site, because everything that would normally fill it is unavailable.

## Primary visitor
A prospect who has read enough to be interested and now wants to know who they would be dealing with.

## Intent
Character assessment. This visitor is deciding whether to trust people, not a service.

## Page archetype
**Philosophy and operating-principles narrative.** The only long-form prose page. Its mechanism is sustained argument rather than modular blocks.

## Primary visual mechanism
**Long-form prose in the `prose` container with principle pull-outs** — short, full-measure statements set larger between passages, carrying the operating principles. The reading experience is a considered document, not a landing page.

## Section order
1. Header
2. **H1 + opening statement** — why the firm exists, in the founder's terms. No eyebrow, no hero band.
3. **Why this approach** — the case for diagnosis before application, as prose.
4. **How we work** — the independent model, in prose rather than steps (How It Works owns the steps).
5. **Principle pull-outs** — interleaved through sections 3–5: honesty, transparency, no overpromising, fair treatment.
6. **Our network** — approved wording only, one short paragraph.
7. **What we are not** — not a bank, not a lender, not a licensed moneylender.
8. **Final CTA** — dark.
9. Footer

## CTA placement
Header; final band. Two placements only — the fewest on the site. A page about integrity undermines itself with a heavy CTA cadence.

## Mobile-specific behaviour
Single column at the `prose` measure. Pull-outs remain full measure, never floated or pulled into a margin. Generous paragraph spacing; this page is read, not scanned.

## Components used
Header · Mobile nav · Prose container · Principle pull-out (typographic pattern) · CTA band · Footer · Links

## Components intentionally not used
**Team biographies and headshots** — not approved · **Case studies** — not approved · Problem-set cards · Timeline · Route-comparison table · FAQ accordion · Calculator · Photography implying office or team scale · Any "coming soon" placeholder module

## SEO / AEO structure
Single H1. H2s as plain statements. Organization schema, consistent with the canonical description. Title direction: *About | The Brokerage Advisory*. Entity consistency: the description here must match LinkedIn, directories and schema word for word.

## Claim / compliance safeguards
**No team biographies until founder-approved bios and proof exist.** No "ex-banker team". No founding date, headcount, transaction volume or years-of-experience claim unless verified. No named clients, lenders or partners. Approved network wording verbatim. The "what we are not" section is mandatory. **No hollow "coming soon" cards** — absent content is absent, not stubbed.

## Placeholder status
`APPROVED CASE STUDY REQUIRED` and team-bio material are both **omitted, not placeholdered**. Per the founder decision, unsupported proof modules do not appear on the public site at all. The page is designed to be complete without them.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Page reads as complete without team bios, case studies or photography
- [ ] No "coming soon" or empty module anywhere
- [ ] No unverified credential, date, headcount or volume
- [ ] Two CTA placements only
- [ ] Prose measure 60–75ch at all widths
- [ ] Entity description matches schema and off-site profiles
