# Construction & Engineering — Page Override

**URL** `/construction-engineering-financing/` · **Archetype** Operating-project cash-gap timeline · Inherits `MASTER.md` and `design.md`

## Page goal
The strongest industry page on the site. Demonstrate, through specificity alone, that the firm understands how a construction or engineering business actually runs — and convert that recognition into an enquiry.

## Primary visitor
The priority ICP: owner-led Singapore construction or engineering business, owner 40–55, revenue roughly S$5–10M, often on site when they open the page.

## Intent
Recognition-seeking. This visitor has been pitched generic finance many times. The page must read as written by someone who has sat in their office.

## Page archetype
**Operating-project cash-gap timeline.** The page is organised around the visitor's operating year, not around financing products.

## Primary visual mechanism
The **full project timeline** (MASTER 5.11, construction configuration): tender and award → mobilisation → progress claims and certification → retention → bond timing → final account and release. The gap between money committed and money received is marked. This is the site's single most persuasive piece of non-claim content. An ordered text list carries the same information in the DOM.

## Section order
1. Header
2. **H1 + lead** — opens on a concrete project situation, not a category label. No eyebrow. This is one of the site's mandated pattern-breaks.
3. **Project timeline** — lead content and primary mechanism.
4. **Where the gap opens** — prose keyed to each stage: plant and materials, subcontractor deposits, manpower, retention held back, bond required before starting.
5. **Situations we are asked about** — new project won, mobilisation funding, existing limits reached, bond requirement, delayed certification, expansion.
6. **What usually needs assessing** — the contract, payment terms, claim and certification history, existing facility letters, retention position.
7. **What this does not promise** — short honesty block.
8. **Route comparison** — shared qualitative table.
9. **FAQ** — construction-specific.
10. **Final CTA** — dark.
11. Footer

## CTA placement
Header; after the timeline explanation; after the situations section; after the route comparison; final band. Five placements — the most of any service page, because this is the priority pathway.

## Mobile-specific behaviour
Timeline becomes a **vertical sequence**, never horizontally scrolling; legible at 320px or replaced by the ordered text list. Light surfaces throughout except the final CTA — this visitor is most likely to be outdoors.

## Components used
Header · Mobile nav · Project timeline (construction configuration) · Route-comparison table and mobile blocks · FAQ accordion · CTA band · Footer · Buttons · Links

## Components intentionally not used
Problem-set cards · Consideration stack · Escalation ladder · Readiness checklist · Inline calculator · Stock construction photography not commissioned for this firm

## SEO / AEO structure
H1 names the industry and the situation together. Question-form H2s on mobilisation funding, progress claims, retention and bonds. Service schema. Title direction: *Construction & Engineering Financing Singapore | The Brokerage Advisory*. Primary AEO cluster for the site.

## Claim / compliance safeguards
**The timeline shows the shape of the problem, not a promised solution.** No figures, no durations presented as typical, no implication that any route closes the gap. No named projects, clients or main contractors. No claim about bond issuance. Register stays operational without becoming loose.
