# Performance Bonds — Page Override

**URL** `/performance-bonds/` · **Archetype** Readiness and requirement checklist · Inherits `MASTER.md` and `design.md`

## Page goal
Explain what a performance bond is in plain English, when one is typically required, and what a business would need to have ready — without implying the firm issues bonds.

## Primary visitor
A contractor or project business that has been told a bond is required, often at short notice, and may not have obtained one before.

## Intent
Urgent and uninformed. This visitor needs teaching, not selling. They arrive with a requirement, not a preference.

## Page archetype
**Readiness and requirement checklist.** A teaching page that ends in a self-check, not a pitch. It is the only page whose primary mechanism is a checklist.

## Primary visual mechanism
A **definition → trigger → preparation checklist** sequence. The checklist is the page's centrepiece: a plain, non-interactive list of what a business would typically need in hand, each item one line with a short clarifier. Presented as an ordered readiness list, not as form inputs and not as cards.

## Section order
1. Header
2. **H1 + plain-English definition** — what a performance bond is, in two sentences. No eyebrow.
3. **When one is typically required** — the trigger situations, as short prose.
4. **Readiness checklist** — lead mechanism. What a business typically needs ready.
5. **What usually needs assessing** — the contract, the obligation, the issuing route, the business's standing.
6. **How we help** — explicitly: we assess and approach, we do not issue.
7. **FAQ** — bond-specific, short.
8. **Final CTA** — dark.
9. Footer

## CTA placement
Header; after the readiness checklist; final band. Three placements — fewer than other service pages, because the page's job is comprehension first.

## Mobile-specific behaviour
Checklist is single column with generous line spacing and no truncation. Definition sits above the fold at 320px. No two-column layout at any width.

## Components used
Header · Mobile nav · Readiness checklist (list pattern) · FAQ accordion · CTA band · Footer · Buttons · Links

## Components intentionally not used
Problem-set cards · Timeline or cycle diagram · Consideration stack · Escalation ladder · Route-comparison table (bonds are not one of the four routes) · Inline calculator · Photography

## SEO / AEO structure
H1 is the reader's question, not a product name. Question-form H2s: what is a performance bond, when is one required, what is needed to arrange one. Answer-first definitions — this page is the strongest AEO candidate on the site. Service schema. Title direction: *Performance Bonds Singapore | The Brokerage Advisory*.

## Claim / compliance safeguards
**No implication of bond-issuance authority.** No bond percentages, no premium rates, no turnaround times, no guarantee of availability. The "how we help" section states the limit of the role explicitly. Checklist items describe preparation, never eligibility.
