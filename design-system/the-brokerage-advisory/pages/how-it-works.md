# How It Works — Page Override

**URL** `/how-it-works/` · **Archetype** Transparent six-step method · Inherits `MASTER.md` and `design.md`

## Page goal
Make the diagnosis-first method concrete enough to be believed, and state plainly what it does not promise. One of the two strongest trust pages.

## Primary visitor
A prospect who is interested but not yet convinced, checking whether there is real method behind the positioning before committing time.

## Intent
Due diligence. This visitor is looking for substance and for the catch.

## Page archetype
**Transparent six-step method.** Sequential and ordinal — the one page where numbered steps are genuinely warranted.

## Primary visual mechanism
A **numbered vertical process spine**: a single continuous rule down the page with six numbered stations attached, each carrying what happens, what is needed, and what it does not mean. Ordinal numbering is intrinsic here, which is why this page may use its eyebrow allowance on step labels.

## Section order
1. Header
2. **H1 + lead** — understand the situation before approaching a financier.
3. **The six-step spine** — lead content and primary mechanism: fact-find · review relevant information · diagnose · discuss possible routes · where appropriate, approach relevant financiers · communicate next steps.
4. **What information may be needed** — plain list, tied to the steps.
5. **What happens after the free assessment** — the honest next-step description.
6. **What this process does not promise** — prominent, at body size, not a footnote.
7. **Final CTA** — dark.
8. Footer

## CTA placement
Header; after the spine; final band. Three placements — the page's job is comprehension, and a heavy CTA cadence would undercut the transparency claim.

## Mobile-specific behaviour
The spine is already vertical. The connecting rule is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Step numbers remain outside the reading flow as text, never as background images.

## Components used
Header · Mobile nav · Process spine (ordered list pattern) · CTA band · Footer · Buttons · Links · Alert block (for the does-not-promise statement, if the visual treatment warrants it)

## Components intentionally not used
Problem-set cards · Timeline or cycle diagram · Route-comparison table · Consideration stack · FAQ accordion · Inline calculator · Photography

## SEO / AEO structure
H1 states the method's principle. Each step is an H2 in plain language. Answer-first throughout. Title direction: *How It Works | The Brokerage Advisory*. Strong candidate for direct quotation by answer engines — each step must make sense extracted alone.

## Claim / compliance safeguards
**Step 5 must not imply approach equals approval.** No timelines for any step. No success language. The does-not-promise section is mandatory, at body size, never collapsed. No claim about lender relationships beyond the approved network wording.

## Placeholder status
None.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Six steps, each stating what it does not mean
- [ ] Does-not-promise section at body size and visually prominent
- [ ] Numbered labels justified as ordinal — the page's eyebrow allowance
- [ ] Three CTA placements only
- [ ] Each step comprehensible extracted standalone
- [ ] No horizontal overflow at 320px
